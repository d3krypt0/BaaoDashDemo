BaaoDash Business Model

A visual, plain-English guide showing how BaaoDash connects customers, merchants, and riders into a sustainable local delivery network.

💡 Core Philosophy: "BaaoDash is not just an app that happens to have riders. It is a local delivery network that uses simple software to organize the riders and businesses that already exist in town."

🎨 Official BaaoDash Community Vision & Campaign Concept Poster

👥 1. The Four Key Players

Who participates in BaaoDash, what they give, and what they get in return.

🛍️

1. Customer

Local resident, student, family, or professional in Baao.

  • Wants: Easy food, medicine, groceries, or sending a quick package.
  • Pay: Item cost + flat local delivery fee.
  • Benefit: No more desperately texting riders one by one.
☕

2. Merchant

Local cafes, bakeries, pharmacies, groceries, & hardware stores.

  • Wants: More sales across town without hiring in-house riders.
  • Action: Accepts order, packs items, hands to rider.
  • Benefit: Instant delivery capability at zero fixed monthly cost.
🛵

3. Rider

Motorcycle riders + traditional habal-habal & pedicab drivers.

  • Wants: Steady daily income and more delivery jobs.
  • Action: Picks up package/food and drops it off safely.
  • Benefit: Retains 75%+ of delivery fee; flexible hours.
⚡

4. BaaoDash

The local orchestrator (Web platform + local dispatch team).

  • Role: Matches orders, guarantees pricing, handles customer support.
  • Revenue: Retains ₱15 to ₱20 margin per delivery.
  • Moat: Knows local streets, barangays, & community trust.

💡 Why This Solves the "Chicken-and-Egg" Problem

Most delivery apps fail because riders leave when there are no orders, and customers leave when there are no riders. The Ice Business guarantees recurring morning deliveries from Day 1. Riders have guaranteed income, which keeps them available on the platform when retail customers want coffee or groceries later in the day!

🤝 4. The Inclusive Dispatch Model (Why BaaoDash Wins Locally)

Big tech apps only work with smartphone-owning motorcycle riders. BaaoDash includes everyone in town.

📱 Modern App Riders

Motorcycle Riders with Smartphones

Use the mobile-friendly web app. Can go online/offline with one tap, view Google Maps navigation, upload photo proof of delivery, and track daily wallet earnings.

Ideal for: Cross-barangay fast food & urgent packages
📞 Traditional Drivers (Assisted Dispatch)

Habal-habal, Pedicabs & Tricycles

No smartphone needed! The central BaaoDash dispatcher calls or SMS messages them, or they check in at a designated local rider hub. The dispatcher manages order updates for them.

Ideal for: Heavy sacks of rice, hardware, & short-distance town trips
📈 5. Growth Flywheel: From Baao to Rinconada

How a focused small-town network scales systematically without burning millions of pesos.

Phase 0 - 1

Manual Validation & Ice Anchor (10–20 orders/day)

Operate via Facebook / Phone / Web MVP. Anchor with the ice plant delivering to 5–10 cafes. Prove that people pay and riders show up.

Phase 2 - 3

Merchant Network & Public Launch (30–50 orders/day)

Onboard top 10 local cafes, bakeries, and pharmacies. Deploy QR codes and store table tents across Baao Poblacion.

Phase 4

Nearby Barangays Expansion (75–100 orders/day)

Expand fixed zones to outer barangays. Introduce structured B2B corporate monthly billing for hardware stores & suppliers.

Phase 5

RinconaDash Regional Expansion

Replicate the proven Baao logistics playbook into neighboring Rinconada municipalities. Become the default logistics spine of the district.
